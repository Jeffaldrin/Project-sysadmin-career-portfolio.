WHAT IS SYSTEM ADMINISTRATION?

System administration is the practice of setting up, managing, securing, and maintaining computers, servers, networks, and services so users can work reliably.

01 / THE DAY-TO-DAY

Roles & responsibilities

A System Administrator helps keep an organization's IT environment available, organized, and secure.
